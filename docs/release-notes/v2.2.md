Pick your widget colour from your wallpaper, and a bigger, calmer 2×2.

### New
- **Widget colour:** Settings › Widgets › Widget colour shows the colours of your wallpaper as swatches, with a live preview of the 2×2. Pick one for the pills and the 1×1 ring, or keep White. Parallax reads the colours from the wallpaper itself, so it works the same whether Nothing's dynamic theme is on or off. Each colour is adjusted to stay readable on dark and light widgets, and your pick stays when you change wallpaper. It replaces 2.1's Wallpaper colours switch.

### Changed
- **The 2×2 widget's digits are larger,** and the three rows spread out so the hours line up with the top pill and the seconds with the bottom pill.
- **The 2×2 has no Reset chip.** Tap the time to start, pause or resume, and tap the pills to open the app. To reset, use the app or Reset in the notification.

### Install
Download the APK below and open it on your phone; it installs over earlier versions and keeps your settings. Requires Android 13+.
