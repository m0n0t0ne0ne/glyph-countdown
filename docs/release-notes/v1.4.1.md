A new app icon.

### Changed
- **New icon: Spokes.** Twelve clock-face spokes, three faded as if time is running out, around a red dot.
  - **Light and dark**: a light version normally, and a black one when your phone is in Dark mode. It switches by itself.
  - **Themed icons**: a single-colour version that Nothing OS tints to match your theme, with the faded spokes kept faint.

### Install
Download the APK below and open it on your phone; it installs over earlier versions and keeps your settings. Requires Android 13+. Your launcher may take a moment, or a restart, to show the new icon.
