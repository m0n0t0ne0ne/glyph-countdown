Ten new ways to show the countdown on the Glyph Matrix, and a double tap on the back to stop the alarm.

### New
- **Countdown style** (*Settings › Glyph Matrix*): keep the original draining ring, or pick one of ten styles, each with a live preview:
  - **Pie**: a solid pie with the numbers cut out of it. As slices disappear, the numbers in the empty part light up again.
  - **Iris**: a shrinking disc. **Tide**: a falling water level with a moving wave.
  - **Spokes**: twelve clock-face spokes. **Ticks**: sixty dots round the edge, the last one blinking.
  - **Orbit**: a dot circling once a minute. **Big**: double-size minutes with the seconds sweeping the edge.
  - **Spiral** and **Rings**: shapes only, no numbers. **Radar**: a bright hand over a dotted field.
  - All of them work on the Phone (3) and Phone (4a) Pro, dim when paused, and drain back toward 12 o'clock like the ring.
- **Double-tap back to dismiss** (*Time's up page › Alarm*): tap the back of the phone twice to stop the alarm. It uses the motion sensor and ignores the alarm's own vibration. On by default.

### Note
Android apps can't see presses of the Glyph Button: Nothing reserves a short press for the Glyph Toy carousel. That's why the double tap uses the motion sensor instead.
