New widgets: the time as an engineering drawing.

### New
- **Isometric Pad widgets.** All three widgets are redrawn as a drafting pad: the time in fine line lettering, drawn as a wireframe solid on an isometric grid. The solid's depth is the time left, so the digits flatten into the page as the timer runs. The 2×2 measures that depth with a red dimension line and lists the time left and the time set in a ruled table; the 2×1 shows the time with the set time beside it; the 1×1 shows one number (minutes, or seconds in the last minute).
- **Widget colour** now retints the grid, the back edges and the dimension line from your wallpaper. **Plain** keeps the drafting blue and red.

### Changed
- **Tapping the widgets:** on the 2×2, tap the time (top) to start or pause, and the table (bottom) to open the app. On the 2×1, tap the time to start or pause, and the set time on the right to open the app. The 1×1 still starts or pauses anywhere.
