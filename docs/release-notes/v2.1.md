A cleaner 2×2 widget, widgets in your wallpaper's colours, and a calmer keypad.

### New
- **Wallpaper colours for widgets:** a new switch in Settings › Widgets. When it's on, the pills and the 1×1 ring take your wallpaper's accent colour (the same one Android's own wallpaper colours use). The digits stay white for easy reading; paused stays grey and time's up stays red. It's off by default.

### Changed
- **The 2×2 widget has no buttons.** Tap the time to start, pause or dismiss, and tap the pills to open the app. A small **Reset** appears only while the timer is paused or after time's up.
- **The typing keypad** uses the app's dot font, with no key backgrounds or border, so the keys sit on the plain background. Each key shows a soft circle when you press it. **0** and **00** are dimmed until you've typed a first digit, since a leading zero does nothing.

### Fixed
- **While typing a time,** the face no longer touches the time chip; there's now a gap between them.

### Install
Download the APK below and open it on your phone; it installs over earlier versions and keeps your settings. Requires Android 13+.
