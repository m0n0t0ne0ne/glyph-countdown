Pick up to dismiss replaces double-tap.

### Changed
- **Pick up to dismiss**: picking the phone up off the table, or turning its screen on (power button, tap or raise to wake), now stops the alarm. It replaces double-tap to dismiss, which was too easy to miss on a table and too easy to trigger by mistake. On by default, under Settings › Time's up.
  - Picked up: when the alarm starts with the phone on a table, it notes its angle; picking it up tilts it, and that stops the alarm within about a quarter of a second. Knocks, the alarm's own vibration and a loud speaker shake the phone but don't tilt it, so they don't count.
  - In a pocket: if the alarm starts with the phone in a pocket or bag, moving about doesn't stop it. Take it out and turn the screen on.
  - The first 2 seconds of the alarm are ignored for the screen, in case the alarm itself wakes it.

### Removed
- Double-tap back to dismiss, and the Record taps troubleshooting switch.

### Install
Download the APK below and open it on your phone; it installs over earlier versions and keeps your settings. Requires Android 13+.
