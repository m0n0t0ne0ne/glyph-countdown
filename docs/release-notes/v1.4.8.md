Adds a troubleshooting recorder for double-tap to dismiss.

### Added
- **Record taps** (Settings › Time's up, under Double-tap back to dismiss; off by default): while it's on, each alarm saves the phone's motion readings, and what double-tap made of them, to your Downloads folder as a small CSV file. It's there to tune double-tap on real taps, since taps on a table aren't registering for everyone yet.

Double-tap itself works exactly as in 1.4.7.

### Install
Download the APK below and open it on your phone; it installs over earlier versions and keeps your settings. Requires Android 13+.
