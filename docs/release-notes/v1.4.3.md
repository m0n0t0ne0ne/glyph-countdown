Double-tap to dismiss now works with the phone lying on a table.

### Fixed
- **Double-tap didn't work face down**: with the phone lying on a table, tapping the back twice didn't stop the alarm; you had to pick it up first. A table pushes back, so a tap there barely moves the phone and the jolt was too small and too brief to register. Taps now count whichever way up the phone is: in your hand, face down or face up.
  - The alarm's own vibration, or picking the phone up, still doesn't count as a tap.

### Install
Download the APK below and open it on your phone; it installs over earlier versions and keeps your settings. Requires Android 13+.
