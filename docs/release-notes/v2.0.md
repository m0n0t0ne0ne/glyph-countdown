Glyph Countdown is now **Parallax**, with a new timer screen, new widgets and a new icon.

### New
- **A new name and icon.** The app is now called Parallax. The icon is half a solid disc beside half a disc of matrix dots, a step out of line: the screen and the Glyph Matrix, seen from two places.
- **The face is the dial.** The timer screen shows a live preview of the Glyph Matrix in the countdown style you picked. Drag round the ring of thirds around it to set the time; the handle turns red while you drag. While the timer runs, the ring and the face drain back toward 12, the way the matrix on the back does.
- **Type a time.** Tap HR, MIN or SEC to pick it, then tap it again to type, as in Google Clock: digits fill in from the right (23 is 23 seconds, 2315 is 23 minutes 15 seconds, 231510 is 23 hours 15 minutes 10 seconds). Overflow rolls up, so 7800 becomes 1 hour 18 minutes. Anything over 23:59:59 shows in red and can't be set.
- **New widgets.** All three sizes show hours, minutes and seconds in dot-matrix digits. Progress is 10 pills, each a tenth of the time you set, emptying a third at a time; empty thirds stay faint. The 1×1 wraps the thirds into a ring around the time.

### Changed
- **One row of chips** under the time: the countdown face, the time's-up alert, and Settings. The Glyph Matrix chip and the gear in the header are gone.
- **The 2×1 widget's start/pause button** is now on the right. Tap anywhere else to open the app, as before.
- The **00** button reads in the regular font, so it's clearer at a glance.

### Install
Download the APK below and open it on your phone; it installs over earlier versions and keeps your settings. Requires Android 13+.
