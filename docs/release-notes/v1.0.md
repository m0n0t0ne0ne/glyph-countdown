First official release of **Glyph Countdown**, a countdown timer for the Nothing Phone (3) and Phone (4a) Pro that shows the time left on the rear Glyph Matrix.

### Features
- **Dial timer**: drag the dot around the tick ring to set hours, minutes or seconds, up to 23 h 59 min 59 s
- **Glyph Matrix countdown**: the time left inside a ring that drains clockwise
  - Phone (3): H:MM or MM:SS on the 25 × 25 matrix
  - Phone (4a) Pro: hours, minutes or seconds on the 13 × 13 matrix
- **Flip to start** and **pocket mode** (the matrix goes dark in a pocket or bag)
- **Alarm sound**: Nothing notification sounds or your phone's alarm sounds, with preview; vibration can be switched off
- **Widgets** in 1×1, 2×1 and 2×2, shaped like Nothing's own, with an optional translucent background
- **Light and dark themes** for the app and widgets: follow the phone, or choose Light or Dark
- **NDot font** on Nothing phones, matching Nothing OS
- **Notification** with a live countdown and Pause / Resume / Reset
