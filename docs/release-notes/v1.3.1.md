The Dim level slider now makes a real difference.

### Fixed
- **Dim level**: high settings now dim the Glyph Matrix properly. LEDs look much brighter than their power level suggests (a quarter of full power looks about half as bright), so the dimming now follows how bright the matrix looks, not just its power level. At 95%, the glow between notes drops from about a quarter of full power to about 4%, and the moment before each note goes nearly dark. The flash on each note stays at full brightness.
- The preview on the Time's up page shows the dimming as it will look on the matrix.

### Install
Download the APK below and open it on your phone; it installs over earlier versions and keeps your settings. Requires Android 13+.
