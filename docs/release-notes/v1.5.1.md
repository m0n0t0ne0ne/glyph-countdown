The Glyph Matrix no longer goes dark for a few seconds as you lay the phone down or pick it up.

### Fixed
- **Dark gap when laying the phone down or picking it up**: laid face down on a table, the matrix lit, went dark for a few seconds, then lit again; picked up with your palm over the screen, it did the same. Covering the front sensor counted as being in a pocket until the phone had proved it was lying still. Now, while the screen faces down, covering the sensor keeps the matrix as it was, so it stays lit from your hand to the table and back.
  - Pockets still stay dark: a phone goes into a pocket upright, so it's dark by then, and stays dark if you sit down and it ends up lying flat.

### Install
Download the APK below and open it on your phone; it installs over earlier versions and keeps your settings. Requires Android 13+.
