The Glyph Matrix now works out for itself where the phone is, so it no longer lights up in your pocket while you're sitting down.

### Fixed
- **Lit in a pocket while sitting**: Pocket mode treated "flat and face-down" as being on a table, and a phone in a pocket can lie that way when you sit. A table is the only place a phone is both flat and perfectly still, so the app now checks for stillness too.

### Changed
- **Pocket mode is gone; it all happens by itself.** Using the proximity sensor, the phone's angle and how still it is:
  - **Lit**: face-down on a table (even with the app open), and in your hand with the screen off.
  - **Dark**: in a pocket or bag (sitting or standing), face-up on a table, and while you use the screen.
- Laid face-down on a table, the matrix lights after about 3 seconds, once the phone has settled. Picked up, it reacts in under a second.
- A knock on the table, or a double tap to dismiss the alarm, doesn't count as movement.

### Install
Download the APK below and open it on your phone; it installs over earlier versions and keeps your settings. Requires Android 13+.
