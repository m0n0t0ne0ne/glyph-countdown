Flip to start lights the Glyph Matrix straight away.

### Fixed
- **Delay after flip to start**: placing the phone face-down with the app open started the timer, but the matrix stayed dark for about 3 seconds, long enough to think it hadn't worked. The app was waiting to prove the phone was lying still on a table before lighting it, and with the screen still on it kept the matrix dark meanwhile. Now the flip itself counts: the matrix lights the moment the timer starts.
  - If you pick the phone back up, or it was in your hand rather than on a table, the matrix follows its usual rules within a second or so.

### Changed
- Flip to start reacts a little sooner: half a second face-down instead of nearly a second.

### Install
Download the APK below and open it on your phone; it installs over earlier versions and keeps your settings. Requires Android 13+.
