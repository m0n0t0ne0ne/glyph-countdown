Small fixes to the 2.0 timer screen, found on a real phone.

### Fixed
- **The alert chip** has room for the sound's full name: the face chip is now only as wide as its label.
- **The hint under the buttons** stays on one line while the timer runs, so the buttons no longer shift up.
- **The typing hint** ("Digits fill in from the right") is short enough to fit under the time.

### Install
Download the APK below and open it on your phone; it installs over earlier versions and keeps your settings. Requires Android 13+.
