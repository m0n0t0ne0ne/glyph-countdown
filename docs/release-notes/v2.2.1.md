Widget colour now shows your wallpaper's colours.

### Fixed
- **Settings › Widgets › Widget colour** said your wallpaper gave no colours. Android blocks the way 2.2 asked for them; 2.2.1 reads them another way, so the swatches now appear.

### Install
Download the APK below and open it on your phone; it installs over earlier versions and keeps your settings. Requires Android 13+.
