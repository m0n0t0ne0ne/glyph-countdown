Double-tap to dismiss works with the phone face down on a table.

### Fixed
- **Double-tap didn't register on a table**: a recording of real taps (thanks to Record taps) showed why. Lying face down, the table stops the phone moving through its screen, so a tap on the back comes out as a sharp jolt along the phone's length instead. Double-tap only looked for jolts through the screen, so it ignored them. Lying flat, a sharp jolt in any direction now counts; in your hand, nothing changes.
- **The alarm's vibration could stand in for a tap**: the jolt of the motor starting or stopping could count as one of the two taps. Those moments are now set aside, so the alarm can't dismiss itself.

### Install
Download the APK below and open it on your phone; it installs over earlier versions and keeps your settings. Requires Android 13+. You can turn Record taps back off.
